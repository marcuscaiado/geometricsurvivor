Play immediately on Mobile, Tablet, or PC:
👉 **[https://marcuscaiado.github.io/geometricsurvivor/](https://marcuscaiado.github.io/geometricsurvivor/)**

---

## 🕹️ Controls (Mobile & Desktop)

- 📱 **Mobile Touch**: Dynamic on-screen Virtual Joystick appears wherever you touch and drag your thumb.
- 🖱️ **Mouse-Only**: Click & drag anywhere on screen to smoothly glide and steer your ship.
- ⚡ **Auto-Targeting & Auto-Fire**: Weapons automatically seek out and blast the nearest geometric enemies within range!
- ⌨️ **Keyboard (Optional)**: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.

---

## ⚡ Features

- 🛸 **3D Neon Arena** — Vibrant glowing wireframes, scanlines, and bloom post-processing
- ⚔️ **Auto-Firing Arsenal** — Plasma blasters, homing missiles, orbiting lasers, and shockwave pulses
- 📈 **Roguelite Upgrades** — Level up to choose weapon evolutions, attack speed, movement buffs, and magnet pickups
- 👾 **Swarming Enemy Waves** — Geometric horde with dynamic pathfinding and boss encounters
- 🔊 **Procedural Audio** — Retro synthesizer SFX for laser shots, explosions, level-ups, and game over
- 📱 **100% Mobile Ready** — Responsive touch joystick and full touch tap support for cards and menus
